TIMELESS TRADITIONS

Sacred Rituals & Their Meaning

🪔

Gowri Puja

గౌరీ పూజ

The bride seeks Goddess Gowri's blessings for harmony, happiness and a beautiful married life.

🌿

Jeelakarra Bellam

జీలకర్ర బెల్లం

Cumin and jaggery symbolize two lives becoming inseparable while sharing both the sweet and challenging moments of life.

🤲

Kanyadanam

కన్యాదానం

A meaningful family blessing representing trust, love and the joining of two families.

🤝

Panigrahanam

పాణిగ్రహణం

The groom holds the bride's hand, symbolizing partnership, companionship and shared responsibility.

📿

Mangalyadharanam

మాంగల్యధారణ

Tying the mangalsutra represents commitment and the sacred marital bond.

🌾

Talambralu

తలంబ్రాలు

The couple showers sacred rice over one another, symbolizing happiness, abundance and prosperity.

👣

Saptapadi

సప్తపది

Seven sacred steps represent promises for partnership, family, strength and a shared life together.

✨

Arundhati Nakshatram

అరుంధతి నక్షత్రం

The couple is shown the Arundhati star, traditionally symbolizing devotion and companionship.

🌸

Appagintalu

అప్పగింతలు

An emotional moment of blessings and transition as the bride begins a new chapter of life.
